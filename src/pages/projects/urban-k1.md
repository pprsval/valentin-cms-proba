### Self-Service and Information Kiosk

A touchscreen self-service and information kiosk constructed from 3, 4, and 8 mm painted sheet metal components.

The design combines minimalist aesthetics with a robust, timeless construction, optimized for ease of assembly. Developed in Autodesk Inventor, the project brings together technical design, documentation, and product visualization.

**Project scope:**

* 3D CAD modeling and assembly design
* Sheet metal design and flat pattern development
* Manufacturing-oriented component design
* Exploded assembly views and parts lists
* Technical illustrations for assembly and user manuals
* Photorealistic renderings for product catalogs and marketing materials

The complete project presentation is available for download in PDF format at the end of the gallery.
